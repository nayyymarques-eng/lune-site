The Sun takes a year to walk around the zodiac. The moon does it in about 27 days. So every month, for roughly two and a half days, the moon passes through the sign you were born under.

Astrologers read these days as a small monthly reset. Feelings sit a little closer to the surface, and your own needs are easier to hear. Nothing is decided for you. The moon is a rhythm you can use as a reminder: a cue to put the phone down, light a candle and check in with yourself.

## Why we read the moon by sign

It is an old habit. Babylonian astronomers divided the sky into twelve equal signs around the fifth century BCE. The same gesture lives in India, in the 27 lunar mansions called nakshatras, and in China, in the 28 lodges, where the moon rests one night at a time. Different skies, one habit: noticing where the moon is tonight.

## How to use it, sign by sign

### Moon in Aries

The tone: a little courage.

- Start the thing you keep postponing, for ten minutes only
- Move your body before you think it through
- Say one honest sentence you have been holding

A 15-minute practice: Light a candle. Write the first step of one plan on paper, then do it before the moon leaves your sign.

### Moon in Taurus

The tone: patience and the body.

- Eat one meal slowly, with no screen
- Tidy one small corner, only one
- Name what is already enough

A 15-minute practice: Hold a warm cup in both hands. Breathe in for 4, out for 6, ten times. Write three things your body did for you this week.

### Moon in Gemini

The tone: a curious mind.

- Send the message you keep drafting in your head
- Write three questions, not answers
- Read something outside your usual

A 15-minute practice: Write for five minutes without stopping, starting with "What I really want to say is". Keep one line, fold the page.

### Moon in Cancer

The tone: tenderness for home.

- Make one room soft: a lamp, not the ceiling light
- Call someone who feels like home
- Let yourself need something, and ask for it

A 15-minute practice: Sit somewhere you feel held. Write the names of three people who make you feel at home, and thank one of them today.

### Moon in Leo

The tone: warmth and play.

- Make something only for the joy of it
- Wear the thing you save for later
- Praise someone out loud, and mean it

A 15-minute practice: Put on one song you love. Write what made you glad to be yourself this month, even if no one saw it.

### Moon in Virgo

The tone: small, careful order.

- Choose one habit, not five
- Clear one surface and leave it clear
- Be as kind to your body as to your to-do list

A 15-minute practice: Clear a small table. Light a candle. Write the one routine that feeds you and the one that only tires you.

### Moon in Libra

The tone: balance with others.

- Notice where you give more than you receive
- Make something beautiful, even a set table
- Ask instead of assuming

A 15-minute practice: Write two lists side by side: what I give, what I receive. Circle one thing to rebalance this week.

### Moon in Scorpio

The tone: depth and honesty.

- Write what you would not say out loud, then fold it away
- Choose one honest conversation
- Let one thing end without explaining it

A 15-minute practice: Dim the lights. Breathe in for 4, out for 6. Write one door you usually keep closed, and one small way to open it.

### Moon in Sagittarius

The tone: a wider view.

- Walk somewhere new, even a new street
- Write what you believe now, not what you used to
- Say yes to one small adventure

A 15-minute practice: Step outside and look at the farthest point you can see. Write one bigger question, and one first step toward it.

### Moon in Capricorn

The tone: steady effort.

- Pick one long goal and only its next step
- Count rest as part of the plan
- Notice what you have already built

A 15-minute practice: Write the goal at the top of a page and the very next step under it. Put the page where you will see it tomorrow.

### Moon in Aquarius

The tone: friendship and freedom.

- Write the idea everyone would call strange
- Reach out to a group you belong to
- Step back and look at the pattern

A 15-minute practice: Write the names of the people who walk with you. Next to one, write a hope you share. Send it to them.

### Moon in Pisces

The tone: softness and dreams.

- Keep a notebook by your bed tonight
- Let music do the talking for ten minutes
- Forgive one small thing, yourself included

A 15-minute practice: Lie down in the dark for ten minutes with slow breaths. When you get up, write the first image that comes to mind.

## A note on science

Large sleep studies have not found that the moon changes how we sleep (Cordi et al., 2014, Current Biology). We use the moon as a rhythm and a symbol, not as a cause. What the research does support are the small gestures inside each practice: slow breathing, writing on paper, giving thanks.
