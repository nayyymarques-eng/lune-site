Every two and a half days, the moon moves into a new sign. It is the fastest clock in the sky, and the oldest one people have read.

## A sign is a tone, not a forecast

When the moon is in Aries, the night carries a little courage. In Taurus, something slower. The moon does nothing by itself: a phase is a moment, the sign a tone, and the house an area of your life. You bring the meaning.

## Where it lands in your sky

The same moon falls on a different part of every life. Count from your Sun sign: if you are a Leo and the moon is in Aries, it falls on your 9th house, the one of far places and big questions. With your birth time, you count from your rising sign instead, and the reading becomes your own.

## When the moon is in your sign

About once a month, the moon passes through your own sign. It falls on your 1st house: your body, your presence, the way you begin. A good night to start something small, or simply to notice how you are.

## An old way of looking

Reading the moon by sign is old. Babylonian astronomers divided the sky into twelve equal signs around the fifth century BCE, and Hellenistic astrologers read each sign as a whole house of life. India keeps a kindred practice in its 27 lunar mansions, and China in its 28 lodges, where the moon rests one night at a time.
