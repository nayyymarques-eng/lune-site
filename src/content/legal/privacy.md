Your sky is personal. This page says what Lune keeps, why, and what you can do about it.

**What we collect**
- **What you tell us:** your name, your email, your birth date, and, if you choose, your birth time and birth place. We use them to read your sky: your Sun sign, your rising sign and your houses.
- **Where you are tonight:** your city or, if you allow it, your approximate location. We use it only to time the moon to your sky. You can type a city instead.
- **What you write in practices:** intentions and notes stay on your phone. If you create an account, they sync to your account so you don't lose them.
- **How the app is used:** simple events, such as "practice completed" or "trial started", so we know what helps. These events never include what you write or your birth data.
- **Purchases:** subscriptions are handled by Apple or Google. We see whether you are subscribed, never your card.

**Who helps us run Lune**
- Supabase: accounts and sync.
- PostHog: usage events, as described above.
- RevenueCat: subscription status.
- Apple and Google: payments and app delivery.

We don't sell your data and we don't share it for advertising.

**Your choices**
- See, correct or delete your data at any time in You › Account, or by writing to [email].
- Deleting your account removes your data from our servers within [30] days.
- You can use Lune without allowing location.

**Children**
Lune is for people 16 and older.

**Changes**
If this policy changes in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app first.
