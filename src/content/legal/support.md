title: "Support"
---

We read every message. Write to **hello@lunerituals.app** and we'll answer within two working days.

**Your subscription**
Payments go through Apple or Google, so changes happen in your store account:
- iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Lune
- Android: Google Play › your profile › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions › Lune

Cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends to avoid the next charge. Refunds follow the store's rules: request them at reportaproblem.apple.com or in Google Play.

**Restore a purchase**
On a new phone, open Lune › You › Account › Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple or Google account.

**Your account and data**
You can see, correct or delete your data in You › Account. To delete everything, see [Delete your account](/delete-account).

**Your safety**
Lune's practices are moments of attention, not treatment. If you are going through something heavy, you deserve a person. In the US, call or text 988. Elsewhere, your local emergency number or a crisis line near you can help.
